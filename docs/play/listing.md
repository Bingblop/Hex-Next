## Full description (max 4000)

```
Hex Next themes the system apps on Samsung Galaxy phones running One UI 7, 8 and 9 (Android 15 to 17). Pick a colour, choose an icon pack, preview it live, and apply it to Settings, Quick Settings, My Files and the rest of One UI in one tap.

WHAT YOU CAN CHANGE
• Colours across One UI system apps, with light and dark variants, built from a single seed colour or your own accents
• Icon packs for the Settings dashboard and My Files, with matching icons generated for newer rows a pack does not cover
• Your own icons: import PNG, ICO, JPG or WebP for any icon slot and Hex Next sizes them for every screen density
• Fonts: set a .ttf or .otf as the system font
• Backgrounds: use your own image for Settings and other themeable backgrounds
• Quick Settings colours, tuned for each One UI version's layout

SEE IT BEFORE YOU APPLY IT
Every change has a live preview in the editor, from icons to fonts to colours.

AI THAT WORKS FOR YOU (OPTIONAL)
Describe a theme in words and Hex Next suggests colours, accents and a pack style. Describe an icon and generate it on the spot. You bring your own API key from the provider you prefer; keys stay encrypted on your phone and are only ever sent to that provider.

SAFE BY DESIGN
• One tap removes everything and restores Samsung's defaults
• A safe-mode watchdog undoes a theme automatically if the phone does not boot cleanly
• Every apply writes a readable report of exactly what changed
• Only Samsung and Android system apps are touched; your other apps are left alone

THEME PACKS
The default theme, custom colours, your own icons, fonts and backgrounds, and the AI features are free. Each theme pack is a one-time in-app purchase through Google Play, or unlock every pack, including packs added later, with a single purchase. Purchases restore on any phone signed in to the same Google account.

REQUIREMENTS
• A Samsung Galaxy phone running One UI 7, 8 or 9 (Android 15 to 17)
• Shizuku (free on Google Play). For full theming, start Shizuku with root
  – Shizuku started with root: per-app colours, icon packs, custom icons, fonts and backgrounds
  – Shizuku over Wireless debugging (no root): your accent colour as the system palette only
Phones from other brands and older One UI versions are not supported; the app tells you so on first launch.

PRIVACY
No account, no ads, no analytics. Your themes, imports and device details stay on your phone. Read the full policy: https://bingblop.github.io/hexnext-site/privacy-policy.html

Hex Next is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Samsung or Google. Samsung, Galaxy and One UI are trademarks of Samsung Electronics Co., Ltd.
```
