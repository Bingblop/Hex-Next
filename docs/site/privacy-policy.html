Hex Next Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026 · Applies to the Android app "Hex Next" (package com.hexnext.themer)

Hex Next is a theming app for Samsung phones running One UI. It is built to work on your device, not in the cloud. This policy explains what the app does with information, in plain terms.

What we collect

We do not require an account, and we do not run analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services. The app does not collect or transmit personal information to us.

What stays on your phone

  • Theme settings you choose (colours, packs, icons, fonts, backgrounds) and the themes the app builds from them.
  • Device scan data: the list of Samsung system apps and their theme resources on your phone, used to build overlays that fit your exact software version.
  • Build logs and reports that describe what the app changed, so you can undo it and diagnose problems. You can share these files yourself; the app never uploads them.
  • Images and fonts you import for custom icons, backgrounds and system fonts. They are stored in the app's private storage until you delete them.
  • AI provider keys you choose to enter. They are encrypted with a key held in your phone's hardware-backed keystore, are excluded from backups, and are never sent anywhere except to the provider they belong to.

Uninstalling the app removes all of this data. "Remove theme" inside the app restores Samsung's defaults.

Optional AI features

If you turn on icon or theme generation, the prompt you type is sent directly from your phone to the AI provider you selected (for example OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, xAI, Perplexity or Stability AI), using your own API key. Those providers process your prompt under their own privacy policies. Nothing is routed through servers operated by us, and the feature is off until you add a key.

Purchases

Theme packs are sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment and handles your payment details; we never see them. To confirm a purchase and protect against tampering, the app sends the Google Play purchase token and a Google Play Integrity token to our licence server, which replies with an entitlement for the pack you bought. The server stores the purchase token, a random app-instance identifier and the entitlement; it does not receive your name, email, contacts or location.

Deleting your data

Everything the app keeps on your phone is removed when you uninstall it or clear its storage. To have the purchase record held by our licence server deleted, email kyle.tameirao@gmail.com from any address with your Google Play order number; we delete it within 30 days and confirm by reply. Deleting the record means the app can no longer restore that purchase.

Permissions

PermissionWhy
InternetDownloading theme packs you own, verifying purchases, and optional AI requests.
Root or Shizuku accessInstalling and removing system theme overlays. Hex Next only touches Samsung and Android system apps and only the resources needed for your theme.
Query installed packagesDetecting which Samsung system apps are present so the right overlays are built and missing (debloated) apps are skipped.

Children

Hex Next is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Security

All network traffic uses HTTPS. Keys and entitlements are stored encrypted. The app verifies its own signature and the integrity of downloaded packs before using them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date. Material changes will also be announced in the app's release notes.
